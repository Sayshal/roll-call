## New Features

- Combat dock shows the turn order as a frameless bar in Blade or Codex style, which you can snap to any screen edge or float.
- Privacy rules per token disposition decide which names, health, initiative and effects players see in the dock and the core tracker.
- Turn clocks count down each turn and can nudge the player or end the turn when time runs out.
- Round events let you schedule reminders or extra turns on specific, repeating or rolled rounds.
- VS screen plays a full-screen allies versus enemies intro in turn order when combat starts.
