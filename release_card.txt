## New Features

- Docks too long for their edge hide the overflow, fade the cut ends and scroll with the mouse wheel instead of shrinking.
- Health labels on tokens scale with the grid and token size, with a per-user size setting.
- Card Art setting shows actor portraits instead of token images on dock cards.
- Up Next label shows a Blade or Codex badge with theme colors or a custom image.
- The On Deck marker is now Up Next and can be an arc, a ring or off.
